How the tournaments work

  1. The TA announces the teams (for example A1, A2, B1).
  2. Open the lab's tournament step (★), enter your name and team, and use Practice run as often as you like. Practice uses practice data; the official run uses different hidden data, so the winning strategy is the robust one.
  3. Official run: once per person. Your settings are sent to the server, which scores them on the hidden data. Nobody, including you, sees that data, so the score cannot be tuned to it or faked.
  4. The live leaderboard updates by itself, by player and by team. The TA projects it.

Everything except the official scoring runs in your browser. No installation, no account, works on phones.
